Kruger National Park All-Inclusive Safari Packages

Our Kruger National Park Inclusive Safari Packages are designed for travelers who would want a seamless, unhurried safari experience in Kruger. Our Safari stays are inside Kruger National Park's classic rest camps, so that we have full access to the early hours of the park without having to wait at a queue.

What is a All Inclusive Kruger Safari?

A all inclusive Kruger National Park safari is a classic Kruger safari experience that allows you to experience Kruger as it is suppose to be experienced, by staying inside the park. Through out my life I have stayed inside the park and have always loved this experience as you get to go out as soon as the gates open and you can stay out until the gates close.

However as this is super popular, making bookings for this type of safari requires us to start booking months in advance to secure a bungalow.
