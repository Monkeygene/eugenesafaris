Kruger National Park

Explore Kruger National Park through its wildlife, landscapes, rest camps, weather, gates and places to stay. Whether you're planning your first visit or returning for another safari, this guide brings together the essential information you need to understand the park and decide where to explore.

Understanding Kruger National Park

Kruger National Park is enormous. Covering around 19,500 square kilometres, it is one of Africa's largest national parks and stretches roughly 360 kilometres from north to south. The park's incredible diversity of landscapes, vegetation and wildlife changes as you travel through it, making the northern, central and southern regions each feel distinctly different.

Beyond Kruger's boundaries, the park also forms part of the much larger Great Limpopo Transfrontier Park, a 35,000-square-kilometre conservation area connecting protected areas across South Africa, Mozambique and Zimbabwe.
