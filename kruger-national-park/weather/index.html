Weather in Kruger National Park

The Weather in Kruger National Park can shift dramatically by season, from lush, greenly painted landscapes and incredible birding during the summer rains, to dry winter mornings where wildlife gather at shrinking waterholes.

In this Kruger National Park weather guide you will find out about temperatures, rainfall, and how these affect wildlife month by month, so you can plan your safari around what matters to you.
