Kruger National Park

Rest Camps in Kruger

I am lucky enough to have visited every main rest camp in Kruger National Park and weigh in my knowledge of each of these beautiful Kruger rest camps. Each of them is unique in location and situated in completely different habitats. Although there are many complaints about these rest camps accommodation, I have seen a surgence in SANParks doing a revamp of their rest camp accommodation which will be excellent.

I have been lucky to take my guests and we stayed in the revamped bungalows at Skukuza Rest Camp. These accommodation were lux and we enjoyed their new take on the classic Kruger bungalows.

Southern Kruger

Southern Kruger is by far the most popular region in Kruger National Park, the south is easily connected by travel. And is also the most difficult to get accomoodation booked but not as difficult as Satara Rest Camp. The Southern parts of Kruger has five important main Rest Camps. Let's start with the most popular camp, Lower Sabie Rest Camp, which is situated in the south east of Kruger National Park next to the Sabie River that flows down into the Crocodile River. Skukuza is the heart of Southern Kruger and is extremely busy rest camp as most operators use this as a base for picnics and is the largest rest camp in Kruger National Park, it hosts a golf course, nursery and 2 restaurants.

Central Kruger

Central Kruger has four of Krugers main rest camps, and 2 of them are very popular rest camps to visit in Kruger. Satara Rest Camp is by far the most popular rest camp in Kruger National Park. Getting a booking secured here takes precicion and planning and sometimes you have to book months in advance. Olifants Rest Camp is second popular in the Central region as it easily connects you to the satara plains that attract people and these plains has the best cat sightings in Kruger especially for lions. Orpen Rest Camp is one of the camps that people don't really know about, but it sits on the western border of Kruger Park and is a very small main rest camp but a good place if you know you'll be running late on your visit to Kruger. Lastly is Letaba Rest Camp and is also a great rest camp especially if you want to stay in one place but visit the north and also want to visit the central areas like satara.

Northern Kruger

The Northern Kruger has three of the Kruger main rest camps, in the far north you have Punda Maria Rest Camp, beautifully situated on a hill and surrounced by dense Mopani Woodland, incredibly beautiful. Punda Maria plays an important base for all the northern birding and if you start your journey in the far north, there is the pafuri camp but I dont think this camp has bungalows. a hundred or so kilomerets south you have Shingwedzi Rest Camp, and this camp is the most popular camp in the northern region. The habitat that surrounds the Shingwedzi River is unique to the whole of Kruger and you won't find a river looking this good in the southern Kruger. The main camp is on the river and has incredible loops in the area. Last is Mopani Rest Camp, the name says it self and this camp is close to where the northern region might start in Kruger. The camp is surrounded by dense Mopani bush.
