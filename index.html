Guided Safaris in Kruger National Park

At Eugene Safaris, we believe the best wildlife encounters can't be rushed. Our owner-guided safaris are built around a simple philosophy: spend more time with wildlife, follow nature's rhythm, and create each day around your interests rather than a fixed schedule.

Whether you're dreaming of a private safari, photographing a leopard in perfect light, or searching for a lifer on a specialist birding tour, every experience is designed to give you the freedom to slow down and truly experience Kruger National Park.

Why We Believe in Slow Safaris

At Eugene Safaris, we believe the best wildlife encounters can't be rushed. Rather than chasing a checklist of sightings, we slow down, observe, and let nature unfold at its own pace. It's often in those quiet moments that Kruger's most memorable experiences happen.

Spending twenty minutes watching a leopard rest in a marula tree, following a family of elephants as they interact around a waterhole, or waiting patiently while alarm calls reveal a hidden predator often creates memories that last far longer than simply driving to the next sighting.

Every safari is owner-guided by Eugene, allowing each day to adapt naturally to the wildlife, the conditions, and your interests. Whether your passion is mammals, birds, photography, or simply experiencing the African bush, the journey is never dictated by a fixed schedule.

If you're looking for a private safari built around this philosophy, explore our Private Kruger Safaris.

Wildlife on Nature's Terms

The most memorable moments in Kruger National Park rarely happen on a schedule. They happen when you're willing to slow down, stay a little longer, and allow nature to unfold in its own time.

We don't measure a safari by the number of kilometres driven or the length of a sightings list. Instead, we focus on the moments that many visitors drive straight past — elephants greeting one another at a waterhole, a leopard resting high in a marula tree, or the alarm calls that quietly reveal a predator hidden nearby.

Every day is shaped by the wildlife, the conditions, and your interests. That's the philosophy behind every safari with Eugene Safaris.

Meet Your Guide: Eugene Booysen

Eugene Safaris is a small, owner-led safari company built around a simple belief: the best wildlife experiences happen when safaris are personal, flexible, and guided by someone who genuinely knows the park.

Having spent years exploring and guiding in Kruger National Park, Eugene combines local knowledge, wildlife expertise, and a relaxed guiding style to create safaris that feel unhurried and authentic. Whether you're hoping to photograph a leopard, find a sought-after bird species, or simply enjoy time in the bush, every safari is tailored around your interests.

Unlike larger operators, you'll deal directly with Eugene from your first enquiry through to your final game drive. No sales teams, no handovers, and no shared safari vehicles — just a private safari experience designed around you.
